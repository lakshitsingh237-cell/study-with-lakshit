Photosynthesis & Plant Stomata

Plants prepare glucose using Sunlight, CO2, Chlorophyll, and Water. Oxygen is released through stomata pores.

• Chloroplast: Contains green pigment Chlorophyll.

• Stomata: Tiny pores guarded by guard cells for gas exchange.

• Chemical Equation: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
